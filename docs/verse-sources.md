# Source registry

Every entry in `Scripts/verse_refs.txt` cites one key from this table. The key
records *why* a verse is in the set, so the selection can be examined rather
than taken on trust.

## An honest statement of what these are

These keys are structural attribution, not scholarly vetting. They say the
verse is a recognised member of a named group, or that it appears under its
theme in a standard topical index of the Qur'an. They do not represent review
by anyone with formal training, and nothing here should be presented as though
they do. If this set is ever to be described as vetted, that is a separate step
and a different person.

## Keys

| Key | Covers | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| `ayat-al-kursi` | 2:255 | The single most widely recited verse outside al-Fatiha, named in the hadith literature and in every standard devotional collection. |
| `khawatim-al-baqarah` | 2:285, 2:286 | The closing ayat of al-Baqarah, a recognised nightly recitation. Note they exceed the caps as a pair and appear as separate entries. |
| `al-fatiha` | 1:1-7 | The opening surah, recited in every unit of prayer. |
| `muawwidhatayn` | 113:1-5, 114:1-6, 112:1-4 | The surahs of refuge and al-Ikhlas, a fixed group in the devotional literature. |
| `luqman-counsel` | 31:12, 31:13-15, 31:16-19 | Luqman's address to his son, a self-contained passage of moral instruction. Split into three entries because the full 31:12-19 runs to 1229 scalars and exceeds every tier cap. |
| `furqan-servants` | 25:63-65, 25:67-70, 25:74 | The closing description of the servants of the Most Merciful. |
| `hujurat-akhlaq` | 49:11-12, 49:13 | The al-Hujurat passage on mockery, suspicion, backbiting, and human equality. |
| `quranic-dua` | every entry themed `dua` | A supplication addressed to Allah in the Qur'an's own words. These form the backbone of standard du'a collections. |
| `topical-index` | the remainder | The verse appears under its assigned theme in a standard topical index of the Qur'an. This is the weakest key in the table and the one most worth revisiting. |

## Revisiting this

`topical-index` carries most of the set. That is a known weakness: it attests
that a verse is conventionally read under its theme, not that this particular
selection of it is the right one. Narrowing that key into more specific ones is
the most useful improvement available to this file.
